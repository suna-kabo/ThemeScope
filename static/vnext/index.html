05 / UNDERSTAND

Methodology

Custom Theme Score

Median Core + Enabler stock returns over 21 and 63 sessions. Score combines cross-theme percentiles at 60% / 40%. At least five valid members and 80% coverage are required.

Synthetic Theme Trend

A chain-linked price index with weights that drift between rebalances. Trend compares the index with EMA21 and EMA50. Bootstrap observations initialize indicators only.

vs Theme Peers

Each stock is compared with the other members of each theme. W combines its excess returns at 60% / 40%. Long requires W > 0; Short requires W < 0.

Multi-theme qualification

Individual lists retain every qualification. Combined placement prefers Core, then stronger directional W, then theme ID. Assigned section is a placement for this list.

Daily eligibility: close ≥ $5, average volume over 10 completed sessions > 500,000 shares, and daily turnover ≥ $10 million. Earnings is optional; unavailable dates are never inferred.

New membership first earns the session after approval. Old holdings earn approval session t; rebalance occurs at t close. Missing or incompatible prior history makes comparisons unavailable.
