    Market

    Market direction

    SPY and QQQ determine Long Bias, Selective Long, Neutral, Selective Short, Short Bias or Unclassified using price trends and constituent breadth in the Market Matrix. IWM supplies small-cap context only.

    Selective states retain the same research side with lower conviction. Neither market direction nor this list evaluates chart setups.

    Benchmark extension

    Extension shows how far SPY and QQQ are from EMA21 relative to Wilder ATR20. It remains context and does not create another stock list.

    Exact rule

    Distance = (adjusted close − EMA21) / ATR20. ATR starts with the mean of 20 true ranges, then uses Wilder smoothing. Absolute distance below 1 is Normal; 1 to below 1.5 is Stretched; at least 1.5 is Extended. Both benchmarks must agree in direction and degree for the combined state. IWM has no vote.

    Themes

    Theme Score

    Theme Score compares the medium-term performance of reviewed themes on a 0–100 scale.

    Exact rule

    Theme 1M and 3M returns are the medians of valid Core and Enabler price returns over 21 and 63 completed sessions. Each needs five valid members and 80% coverage. Theme Score = 60% of the 1M cross-theme percentile + 40% of the 3M percentile. Related relationships and thematic ETFs do not enter calculations.

    Leading, Improving, Fading, Lagging

    States describe relevance for the current research side. Long leaders have high raw structural scores; Short leaders have low scores. Improving means the move favors that side before leadership. Fading means established directional leadership is deteriorating; it stays researchable. Lagging means the theme does not favor that side or improve materially.

    The current snapshot supplies the exact versioned research rules.

    Neutral and Unclassified have no directional states or research list. Five-session comparisons use a declared reviewed-membership basis and never create official history. Unavailable comparisons explain their cause. Breadth never gates the list.

    Price context

    The synthetic chain-linked theme index keeps its reviewed, effective-dated holdings. Above both EMA21 and EMA50 is Bullish; below both is Bearish; otherwise it is Mixed. Price context is separate from researchability.

    Theme participation

    Participation shows how many valid members are above EMA21, alongside the percentage and sample size. Small samples are explicitly marked and remain context. Participation does not change Theme Score or remove a researchable theme.

    5-session Change and Research Priority

    5-session Change is the current raw Theme Score minus the raw score five completed sessions earlier. The sign stays the same for every market side. State interprets that change for the current Long or Short side. Research Priority is the current research order.

    Stocks

    Compared with theme peers

    Peer strength orders stocks within each theme. Positive means stronger than peers; negative means weaker. Long-side research puts higher values first; Short-side research puts lower values first. Opposite-sign stocks stay visible, and unavailable comparisons come last.

    Exact rule

    Leave the stock itself out of each Core + Enabler peer median. Peer strength (W) = 60% of 1M excess return + 40% of 3M excess return, expressed in percentage points. Each theme relationship has its own comparison.

    Daily liquidity

    The current snapshot supplies the completed-session price and average dollar-volume rule.

    Average dollar volume is the mean of each session’s unadjusted close multiplied by its volume. Missing or invalid completed bars make daily eligibility unavailable. Daily eligibility never changes stable theme membership.

    Daily Research List

    Every daily-eligible Core and Enabler member of a researchable theme enters the list. Theme relevance and state set the order first, then peer strength, then a deterministic ticker tie-break. Neutral market direction produces no directional research list.

    Multiple themes, Saved and export

    A stock may have several genuine theme relationships. The Theme filter shows that specific peer comparison. TradingView export uses the full session list in research order; the highest-priority theme supplies a ticker’s first occurrence. Only reviewed TradingView identifiers are exported.

    Saved is stored on this computer and shared across browsers and ports. Removing a stock from today’s list does not remove it from Saved.

    Research universe

    The manually maintained Base Market Universe covers NASDAQ and NYSE common stocks with market capitalisation above $1 billion and price above $5. Theme research uses genuine reviewed relationships within supported themes. Unmapped stocks are valid. Benchmark constituents are market context, not automatic research candidates.

    Data

    Completed-session data

    The whole workspace uses a completed US market session. A failed refresh preserves the prior successful snapshot. Research and market data stay on this computer. Provider credentials never enter the browser.

    History and provenance

    Official theme indices began at the November 6, 2026 close. Return history starts on November 9. Recent-change comparisons require compatible completed sessions; earlier ETF history remains separate.
