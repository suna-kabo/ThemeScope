Methodology

Market

01 Market Bias / Market Matrix

SPY and QQQ price states and their constituent breadth determine structural Market Bias using the frozen Market Matrix. Both prices Bullish or both Bearish establish unanimous direction; breadth adds context. Other combinations follow the same Matrix. Bias prioritises research; it does not assess entries.

02 Market Extension

For SPY and QQQ separately: (completed close − EMA21) / Wilder ATR20. True Range is the maximum of high − low, |high − previous close| and |low − previous close|, on consistently split/spin-off-adjusted prices. ATR starts with the mean of the first 20 True Ranges, then applies Wilder smoothing: (19 × prior ATR + TR) / 20. Absolute distance below 1 ATR is Normal; 1 to below 1.5 is Stretched; at least 1.5 is Extended. The sign supplies Upside or Downside. Both benchmarks Extended on the same side gives Extended. Both at least Stretched on the same side gives Stretched. Other complete combinations give Normal; missing history or zero ATR gives Unavailable. No IWM, EMA50, breadth or scores enter Extension.

03 Research Focus and index roles

SPY and QQQ determine Bias and Extension. IWM is small-cap context only, with no breadth or influence on either. Under Short Bias, Downside Stretched adds Countertrend Watch and Downside Extended highlights Countertrend. Under Long Bias, the upside equivalents add Pullback-Risk Watch or Highlighted. Other directional combinations favour Directional; non-directional Bias favours observation. Extension changes prominence, never qualification. EMA relation changes are shown only against a compatible previous completed session; no change is inferred from missing or launch-only history.

Themes

04 Theme Strength Score

0–100 relative strength across active themes. Higher means stronger. Theme 1M and 3M are median Core + Enabler price returns over 21 and 63 completed sessions; at least five valid members and 80% coverage are required. Theme Strength Score = 60% 1M cross-theme percentile + 40% 3M percentile. It is not expected return, probability or Research Priority. Related members and thematic ETFs do not enter calculations.

05 Theme Trend

The chain-linked synthetic index has drifting weights between rebalances. Its level above both EMA21 and EMA50 is Bullish, below both is Bearish, and other combinations including equality are Mixed. Synthetic index history is distinct from median member returns.

06 Theme breadth

Members above EMA21 and members above EMA50 are separate breadth measures. At least five valid Core + Enabler members and 80% coverage are required; each needs a valid current close and corresponding EMA. Breadth is context only and does not affect Theme Strength Score or candidate qualification.

07 Theme Rank and Rank Change

Canonical strength ranks cover the complete Active theme universe with valid scores, regardless of search, filters or table order. Equal scores share competition ranks: 1, 2, 2, 4. Stable theme ID orders tied rows without implying greater strength. Rank Change compares the previous compatible adjacent completed session. No change is inferred when prior history, scores or a stable universe are missing. A newly active theme or changed scored universe has no comparable rank change.

08 Research Priority

Research Priority describes Directional theme relevance under the current Bias. It is not strength rank: the strongest theme may be Watch under Short Bias. Research Priority Change compares the previous compatible completed session. Core business directly participates in a theme; Supporting business supplies products or services that enable it. Untracked Concepts have no active calculation basket.

Stocks

09 Relative Strength vs Theme Peers (W)

Weighted 1M/3M excess return versus the other Core + Enabler members of the same theme, leaving the stock itself out. W = 60% 1M return vs peers + 40% 3M return vs peers. Positive means stronger than theme peers; negative means weaker. Unit: percentage points. W is theme-specific, not expected return or a trading signal.

10 Liquidity eligibility

A completed regular-session trading bar, close ≥ $5, 10-completed-session average volume > 500,000 shares, and daily turnover ≥ $10 million are required. Liquidity eligible is distinct from Meets candidate criteria. Missing trading bars cannot be replaced by a merger valuation for stock research. Earnings are optional and never determine qualification.

11 Directional Candidates

Existing Directional rules are unchanged. Long: Theme Strength Score ≥ 50 with Bullish or Mixed trend, or a valid Newly bullish exception; W > 0. Short: score < 50 with Bearish or Mixed trend, or a valid Newly bearish exception; W < 0. Valid score, current trend, liquidity and existing eligibility requirements still apply. Zero W never qualifies.

12 Countertrend Candidates

Under Short Bias, use Active themes with score < 50 and Bearish or Mixed trend, Core + Enabler relationships and normal liquidity. Resilient (W > 0): stocks showing relative strength inside themes that remain structurally weak. Snapback (W < 0): stocks showing relative weakness inside weak themes and therefore potentially capable of a sharp mean-reversion move. Under Long Bias, mirror this with score ≥ 50 and Bullish or Mixed trend: W < 0 is Relative Weakness; W > 0 is Strong Leaders / Pullback Watch. Pullback Watch is a warning against chasing an extended market, not a W-based pullback prediction. Positive W sorts descending, then score descending; negative W sorts ascending, then score ascending; ticker and theme ID break display ties. No new score is introduced. Ordering reflects relative strength or weakness, not reversal probability. Neither group confirms a reversal or entry. Chart confirmation is performed separately by the user. Both lenses remain inspectable in Normal conditions.

13 Multi-theme relationships and exports

A stock may qualify independently in multiple themes. Stocks view shows one representative relationship; Grouped by theme retains relationships. The existing Directional TXT exports are unchanged: individual files keep each qualification and the combined file emits a ticker once. Placement prefers Core, then stronger directional W, then theme ID. Assigned theme is export placement, not a permanent home. Filters and Countertrend do not change exports. Saved is stored in this browser only, independently of automatic candidates.

System

14 Completed-session data

Research uses completed US trading sessions supplied by Alpaca SIP. EMA relation changes and strength-rank changes use stored compatible snapshots, not the browser’s current table order. Missing data is Unavailable; no dates, bars or transitions are fabricated.

15 Launch and history lifecycle

Official indices initialize at exactly 100 at the approved 2026-11-06 close. The first earned session is 2026-11-09. Old holdings earn approval session t; rebalance occurs at t close; new holdings first earn the next trading session. effective_from is the first earned session and effective_to is exclusive. The first possible adjacent-live comparison is 2026-11-10, subject to compatible successful data. Earlier indicator history cannot invent official returns or transitions. Approved terminal corporate actions affect existing-holding accounting only, never tradeability.

16 Public snapshots and publication

A public snapshot fixes market data at one completed US session. Navigation, inspectors, display preferences, browser-local Saved and TXT downloads remain interactive. The site does not refresh market data or hold credentials. Local refresh publishes one complete SQLite snapshot transactionally; a failed refresh preserves the previous successful dashboard and exports. Operational diagnostics remain in Snapshot details.
