How to read EMA, breadth and coverage

EMA21 / EMA50
21- and 50-session exponential moving averages. “Above” means the latest close is higher than that average; the distance is shown as a percentage.

Breadth
The share of constituents with usable prices that close above their own EMA. Example: 355 / 499 = 71.1% above EMA21.

Price coverage
How much of the eligible index has usable price history: 499 / 500 = 99.8%. Coverage is data completeness; breadth is participation among the names with data.

CURATED ETF EXPOSURES

Themes

Industry and thematic ETFs selected by mandate, with ETF holdings breadth and relative returns.

Score method and data terms

CurrentStrength = 70% 1M excess-return percentile + 30% holdings breadth above EMA21. MediumStrength = 70% 3M excess-return percentile + 30% holdings breadth above EMA50. ThemeScore = 60% CurrentStrength + 40% MediumStrength. At least ten valid ETF peers and valid breadth are required; missing inputs are not filled in. Scores are fixed browsing conventions, not a validated trading model.
