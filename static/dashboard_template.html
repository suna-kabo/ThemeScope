Calculation and interpretation notes

Return and excess

For an n-session return, the price source uses adjusted closes at benchmark-session endpoints t and t−n. Exact endpoints are sufficient; any interior gaps are disclosed. Excess is asset return minus benchmark return in percentage points. Relative wealth change is a separate ratio and is not substituted for excess.

Price trend and liquidity

EMA trend uses split-adjusted closes, 21- and 50-session recursive EMAs, five-session EMA21 slope, and a 200-consecutive-session warm-up for full classification. ADV$30 is the mean of raw contemporaneous close × raw share volume across 30 sessions; later split adjustments are reversed to keep dollars consistent.

Corporate actions and coverage

Yahoo adjusted close supplies the total-return-compatible series, including cash dividend reinvestment as defined by the provider. Ordinary dividends are not added a second time. Holdings weights remain percentages of fund net assets and are not renormalized. Global fund holdings only contribute where the issuer and US listing pass evidence and data checks.

Limits

Labels describe observed price behavior, not expected returns. Themes overlap, constituent versus ETF returns are mechanically related, and current holdings cannot establish point-in-time historical membership. The prototype has no persistent database, scheduled task, backtest, or trade signal.
