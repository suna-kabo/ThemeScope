# Product goals

ThemeScope supports a repeatable Market → Themes → Stocks research workflow using
completed US sessions. Gate E real chronological acceptance is **CLOSED**; see the
[closure record](docs/operations/gate-e-closure.md). These remain standing quality goals:

1. Custom Core/Enabler themes drive deterministic medians, Strength, synthetic Trend
   and leave-one-out peer comparisons; thematic ETFs have no calculation role.
2. SPY/QQQ retain the frozen Market Matrix; IWM supplies context only.
3. Normal refresh needs no CSV uploads. Historical SIP, incremental observations and
   immutable provenance support complete, transactional, replay-safe snapshots.
4. Membership and SPY/QQQ rosters remain reviewed and effective-dated; daily liquidity
   never rewrites membership or history.
5. Directional/Countertrend, multi-theme research, deterministic TXT exports and
   browser-local Saved remain clear, accessible and consistent.
6. Windows launch, fixture mode and offline regressions protect first use and failed
   refresh recovery. Optional earnings never block market data.
7. Legacy history stays readable and separate. Secrets, private DBs and raw provider
   data stay outside Git; publication is an explicit frozen-static workflow.
8. No execution, technical-entry signals, IEX fallback, scraping or silent methodology
   changes. No deployment without explicit authorization.
