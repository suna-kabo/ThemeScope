# ThemeScope

Offline manual-CSV research dashboard. The user scans ETF relative momentum and participation, reads deterministic Market and research-priority explanations, then examines configured stocks against their selected ETF. This is an Operate surface: precision and task continuity take precedence over spectacle. Research priorities are not entry signals.

Inputs remain five explicit TradingView CSV roles and a declared US session. Trading methodology, memberships, atomic publication and local rank-history safeguards remain authoritative. No remote assets, live collection, external storage, or new indicators are part of the interface.
