# Market → Themes → Stocks

ThemeScope organizes end-of-day research. Its outputs select research context;
they do not confirm a trade, technical entry or expected return.

## Overview and Market

Read **Market Bias → Extension → Market focus** in the compact direction plane.
SPY/QQQ price state and their separate constituent breadth supply Market Matrix
evidence. IWM is secondary small-cap context: no roster, breadth vote or decision role.
Extension is additional ATR-based context, not a replacement for Market Bias.

## Themes

Compare Active themes by Strength, canonical Strength Rank, Trend and Research
Priority. Search, sorting and filters do not recalculate rank. Breadth is supporting
context and may be unavailable even when other metrics are valid. The Theme Returns
Map uses existing 1M/3M data; all observations remain accessible through selection.
Untracked Concepts have no calculation basket and are not empty Active themes.

Theme names and ticker names are interactive, with persistent link/focus treatment.
Open the right-side Research Inspector to retain the underlying workspace, or
choose **Open full theme** for the complete member table. Full Theme separates
headline Strength/Rank/Priority/Trend, return/breadth evidence and raw index/EMA values.

## Candidates

**Directional** follows the approved Market Bias / theme priority / liquidity /
peer-relative qualification rules. **Countertrend** is a separate lens over the
approved supplied relationships. It can be inspected even when Extension is Normal.
Market context stays labelled independently from the selected lens.

In Short Bias, Countertrend separates peer outperformers from weak-theme laggards.
In Long Bias it separates Relative Weakness from Strong Leaders / Pullback Watch.
W measures strength versus peers; positive W does not predict a pullback. The
Pullback Watch wording is a market-extension warning against chasing.

**Stocks** presents representative relationships; **Grouped by theme** retains
all qualifying relationships without repeating the group Theme in every child row.
Selecting a Theme filter displays that relationship's Role and Peer delta, and
carries its context into inspection. A stock can qualify independently in several themes.

Inspectors retain useful context during polling. Browser Back / nested Back returns
to the prior sheet; Escape closes the owned sheet chain. Search, View, scroll and
trigger focus are restored. Save is an independent control.

## Saved and exports

Saved belongs to this browser and origin, not a cloud account. Its ticker storage
contract remains `themescope-vnext-watchlist`; optional research context is stored
separately. Saving does not change eligibility or qualification.

TXT downloads use published results and verified TradingView identifiers, not
visible table sorting/search filters. Directional and Countertrend downloads remain
separate; combined files deduplicate deterministically. They are watchlists, not orders.
Earnings are optional. No standalone ticker returns or chart data are invented.

## Display and snapshot state

Display controls offer compact/comfortable density, 100–200% text, high contrast
and reduced motion. Keyboard focus is visible; native table layouts progressively
reflow at narrow logical widths. Current selection does not rely on motion alone.

Always read the **completed session** and refresh state. Local Refresh retrieves
new data; fixture mode is fictional; a public snapshot is frozen/read-only. Lost
connection or a failed refresh preserves last committed data and says so. Consult
[methodology](../methodology/README.md) for exact numbers and unavailable states.
