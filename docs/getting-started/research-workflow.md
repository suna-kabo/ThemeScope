# Market → Themes → Research list

Open ThemeScope after market close. The completed-session date identifies the
data being researched. A failed refresh keeps the preceding successful snapshot.

**Overview** shows market direction, whether it changed, themes gaining or losing
strength, and today's research-list size. SPY/QQQ drive the Market Matrix; IWM is
context. Extension remains supporting evidence.

**Themes** compares structural strength, five-session change, participation and
trend. Leading, Improving and Fading themes deserve research in the current
direction. Fading retains established leaders during pullbacks. Search/sorting
never recalculate ranks. Participation shows its sample count and a small-sample
warning where appropriate.

Open a theme or stock inspector without losing your place. Full Theme shows
member relationships, daily eligibility and peer priority. Browser Back returns
through nested context; Escape closes the inspector and restores focus.

**Research list** is the chart-review queue. Stocks deduplicates; By theme retains
independent relationships. Peer strength compares members with their own theme.
Opposite-sign stocks stay visible. ThemeScope does not judge chart setups.

TradingView TXT exports the complete session list in research order, independent
of visible filters. Combined output deduplicates at the highest-priority theme;
theme files retain their own eligible members. These are watchlists, not orders.

**Saved** belongs to this browser and origin. Existing tickers/theme context
survive the redesign. Display controls offer density, text size, contrast and
reduced motion.

**Refresh Data** under **Snapshot and refresh** retrieves local data. Fixture/demo
data are synthetic; public snapshots are frozen and read-only. Exact rules and
diagnostic evidence are in [Research Logic v2](../methodology/research-v2.md).
