# Final frontend rebuild

## Protected boundary

The committed dashboard snapshot, `workspace.js` research adapters, local API,
Saved storage keys, hash aliases and inspector history, and full-session export
contents remain authoritative. No calculation, eligibility, membership, provider,
accounting, or research order changes belong to this work.

## New information architecture

- Market: one compact bias statement, SPY/QQQ evidence, secondary IWM context,
  then a direct continuation into theme research.
- Themes: five-session gains and losses for the current research side, followed
  by the complete sortable theme ledger. Relative state and price direction
  have distinct headings and visual treatments.
- Primary: an ordered stock queue with theme context and peer strength;
  relationship view, filters, sorting and complete exports stay available.
- Saved and Methodology are utility destinations. Snapshot provenance and refresh
  share a disclosure. Browser preferences supply text scaling and reduced motion.
- Contextual research opens in a modal sheet; full theme routes remain valid.

Changes reach current vnext HTML/CSS/JS, font serving and static asset pinning,
current browser acceptance checks and validation documentation. Legacy dashboard,
inspection fixtures and backend research remain untouched. Review synthetic full
universe data at 1920/1440/1280/768/430/390/360/320, all routes and inspectors,
