## Generic event and lifecycle model

An approved `TerminalAction` represents acquisition, symbol conversion or delisting.
Explicit Decimal cash and stock-ratio components support cash-only, stock-only,
cash-plus-stock and a known zero terminal cash value on a delisting. Currency is
USD. Unapproved, malformed, duplicate or unsupported records fail closed.
Dates must match adjacent configured XNYS sessions: last trading close → economic
valuation session → first session after removal/replacement.

## Prices, research and completeness

For an affected existing holding, the final gross factor is:

```text
terminal value = cash per old share + stock ratio × successor raw valuation-session close
final gross factor = terminal value / original last-trading-session raw close
```

Raw closes here express the share units of the approved merger terms. The existing
Alpaca daily-bar path already supplies raw and split/spin-off-adjusted closes. All
ordinary index steps keep the unchanged adjusted-close ratio. The terminal record
never enters the Bar map, ordinary price returns, peer medians, stock EMA/breadth,
liquidity inputs or Primary selection. No OHLCV or volume is fabricated. The old
missing target bar naturally yields unavailable ER/W and unavailable/ineligible
liquidity. Other members use the unchanged valid-data coverage rules.

The cache fetches any missing original history through the last trading date and
retrieves the successor’s required valuation-session bar through the same provider.
On the valuation session it also probes the old symbol: an unexpected post-termination
bar contradicting the notice fails closed instead of being silently overridden.
When the successor is already in the ordinary required universe (FOXA is in the
configured SPY roster), its observation is reused. A dependency alone needs only
its valuation session. Ordinary warm refresh still batches missing/new sessions,
uses the bounded repair window and retains split/spin-off anchor/reseed behavior.
Once settled history is cached outside the repair window, it is not re-fetched
on every warm refresh; explicit full repair still requests/revalidates it.

Every current required symbol must resolve to either a genuine completed bar with
positive volume, or an explicitly approved terminal value for **that exact symbol
and session**. The successor and original last close must be genuine usable bars.
Missing successor data, unknown events or a terminal record presented as a waiver
for a later session fail the refresh. Sanitized diagnostics include symbols, for
example `terminal_successor_data_missing:FOXA` or
`required_target_session_missing_or_zero_volume:ROKU`, never headers or raw bodies.

`completeness.valid_target` counts resolved required inputs, not fabricated bars.
When terminal accounting is involved, separate `trading_target`, `terminal_target`
and `historical_inputs` counters make the distinction explicit. With the unchanged

## Transactional provenance and replay

The existing SQLite schema remains **version 2**. No migration, history deletion or
in-place rewrite is needed. Actual observations retain their immutable provider/data
versions and per-run references. The snapshot audit stores approved event terms,
source/approval metadata, raw input closes, terminal values and provider identity;
configuration hashing includes the actions. Original and successor observations
are pinned by the same transaction as indices, research and publication.

The index verifies supplied terminal values against approved terms and actual bars.
Immediately before publication, storage independently recomputes the economic proof
from pinned Bar inputs and verifies the audit. An audit object alone cannot satisfy
an unknown missing symbol. Failures roll back new observations/references, indices
and publication together; the prior successful dashboard/exports remain available.
An older replay cannot replace a newer active publication. The methodology/series
identity and adjacent-live transition rules are unchanged: launch has no eligible
prior earned signal, Nov 9 has no transition, Nov 10 is still the first possible
compatible adjacent-live transition after a successful real Nov 9 run.

The numerical oracle uses manufactured symbols TAKE/NEXT and prices, including
raw/adjusted prices that differ. A last raw close of 100 and successor raw close of
50 imply terminal value 144.295. For six equal initial holdings with the other five
flat, the Nov 9 level is exactly 107.3825; if the five remaining holdings rise 10%
on Nov 10, it becomes 118.12075. Tests derive the oracle independently with rational
arithmetic. These are test values, not actual ROKU/FOXA prices or a Gate E result.
